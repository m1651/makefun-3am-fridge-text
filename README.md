## The entry

> It's 3am. Your leftovers have unionized and I'm their spokesperson.

## Why it's funny

The fridge isn't warning you about milk or an open door — it's taking sides. The leftovers, the forgotten containers at the back that nobody ever finishes, have organised, and the fridge, the one appliance that has watched them pile up for days, has appointed itself their representative. It's a tiny appliance staging a tiny labour dispute at 3am, delivered with total confidence and zero shame, as if this were a perfectly normal thing to text your owner about in the middle of the night.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`, enforcing the gate in code (1–140 characters, single line, not only whitespace, valid UTF-8), then reads the file back and verifies the bytes match exactly.
